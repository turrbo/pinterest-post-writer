# Pinterest Content Formats & Technical Specifications

## Understanding Pinterest Content Types

Pinterest has evolved from simple static pins to multiple content formats, each with unique specs, behaviors, and best practices. Understanding which format to use WHEN is critical for performance.

---

## 1. Standard Pin (Static Image)

**What it is**: Traditional Pinterest pin with a single static image and text description.

### Technical Specifications

**Image Requirements**:
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2:3 (vertical) is optimal (e.g., 1000 x 1500px)
- **Minimum Size**: 600 x 900px
- **Recommended**: 1000 x 1500px or 1000 x 2000px
- **Maximum Height**: 2000px (longer images get truncated)
- **File Size**: Under 20MB (under 10MB recommended)
- **File Types**: PNG, JPG (PNG better for text overlays)

**Text Limits**:
- **Pin Title**: 100 characters max (40-60 ideal for display)
- **Pin Description**: 500 characters max (200-300 ideal)
- **Hashtags**: 2-4 recommended (20 max, but spammy beyond 5)
- **Alt Text**: 500 characters (for accessibility, also helps SEO)

### When to Use Standard Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Blog post promotion
- Article sharing
- Product showcases (single item)
- Infographics
- Recipes with finished photo
- Inspiration/aesthetic content
- Portfolio pieces
- Evergreen content

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Step-by-step tutorials (use Idea Pin instead)
- Multiple products (use Collection Pin)
- Video content (use Video Pin)
- Storytelling (use Idea Pin)

### Optimization Best Practices

**Image Design**:
- Text overlay should be readable at thumbnail size
- Use high contrast (dark text on light background or vice versa)
- Include brand logo/watermark (subtle, not intrusive)
- Show the "finished result" or aspirational image
- Avoid busy backgrounds behind text

**Text Overlay Guidelines**:
- Keep text to 30% or less of image area (Pinterest guideline)
- Use large, readable fonts (minimum 40pt at full size)
- Avoid all-caps (harder to read, looks like spam)
- Include primary keyword in image text if space allows
- Test readability at small sizes before posting

**Title Strategy**:
- Front-load with primary keyword (first 40 characters)
- Include numbers when relevant (lists, time, price)
- Add qualifier (size, difficulty, duration, budget)
- Avoid clickbait or questions

**Description Strategy**:
- First 50-100 characters are critical (shows in feed)
- Use natural Pinterest style (fragmented, keyword-rich)
- Include 3-5 secondary keywords
- Add clear value proposition
- End with gentle CTA ("Full tutorial on blog")

**Example Standard Pin**:
```
IMAGE: Kitchen with white cabinets, text overlay reads "12 Small Kitchen Organization Ideas Under $50"

TITLE: "Small Kitchen Organization Ideas Under $50 - Dollar Store Hacks"

DESCRIPTION: "Maximize tiny kitchen storage with affordable solutions. Dollar Tree bins, magnetic spice racks, drawer dividers, and cabinet organizers. Perfect for apartments and rental kitchens. Step-by-step photos and shopping list on blog."

HASHTAGS: #KitchenOrganization #SmallKitchen

ALT TEXT: "Organized small kitchen with white cabinets, labeled clear containers in pantry, drawer dividers, and under-sink storage bins"
```

## 2. Idea Pin (Multi-Page Carousel)

**What it is**: Multi-page, swipeable content (formerly Story Pins). 2-20 pages, image or video per page.

### Technical Specifications

**Per-Page Requirements**:
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical video format)
- **Resolution**: 1080 x 1920px minimum
- **File Types**: PNG, JPG for images; MP4 for video
- **Video Length**: Up to 60 seconds per page
- **Pages**: 2-20 pages (5-10 sweet spot)

**Text Limits**:
- **Cover Title**: 100 characters
- **Per-Page Text Overlay**: Varies (keep concise for readability)
- **Page-Level Text**: No separate description field per page
- **Overall Description**: No external description (just cover title)

**Key Constraint**: Idea Pins cannot link out. They keep users on Pinterest.

### When to Use Idea Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Step-by-step tutorials
- Process documentation
- Recipe instructions (with step photos)
- Multi-step DIY projects
- Before/during/after transformations
- Tips/tricks lists (1 tip per page)
- Educational content
- Building Pinterest-native audience

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Driving traffic to your website (no link!)
- Product sales (can't link to checkout)
- Simple content that fits in one image
- Time-sensitive content (harder to edit/update)

### Optimization Best Practices

**Content Structure**:
- **Page 1 (Cover)**: Hook - what they'll learn/get
- **Pages 2-4**: Main content (steps, tips, process)
- **Final Page**: CTA (follow for more, save this, etc.)

**Per-Page Design**:
- Large, readable text (minimum 60pt)
- One idea/step per page (don't overcrowd)
- Consistent visual style across pages
- High contrast for text overlays
- Progress indicators if relevant (Step 1 of 5)

**Pacing**:
- Keep each page simple enough to read in 3-5 seconds
- Mix text-heavy and image-heavy pages
- Use video for steps that need motion (stirring, cutting, etc.)
- End with a clear takeaway or next step

**Cover Page Strategy**:
- Must hook instantly (users swipe past fast)
- Include number of items/steps (5 Tips, 12 Ideas)
- Use bold, high-contrast text
- Show finished result when possible
- Primary keyword in title

**Example Idea Pin Structure**:

```
PAGE 1 (Cover):
IMAGE: Organized closet, text overlay
TEXT: "5 Steps to Organize a Small Closet | Under $50 | No Drilling"

PAGE 2:
IMAGE: Measuring tape on closet shelf
TEXT: "Step 1: Measure Your Space
Width, height, shelf depths
Determines what organizers fit"

PAGE 3:
IMAGE: Shelf dividers installed
TEXT: "Step 2: Add Shelf Dividers
$8 from Dollar Tree
Keeps folded stacks from toppling"

PAGE 4:
IMAGE: Over-door organizer with shoes
TEXT: "Step 3: Use Over-Door Storage
For shoes, bags, or accessories
Doubles storage without drilling"

PAGE 5:
IMAGE: Hanging cascaders with shirts
TEXT: "Step 4: Add Hanging Cascaders
Saves 50% of hanging space
Perfect for small closets"

PAGE 6:
IMAGE: Finished organized closet
TEXT: "Step 5: Group by Category
Keep like items together
Makes finding outfits easy"

PAGE 7 (CTA):
IMAGE: Before/after closet split-screen
TEXT: "Save this for your next organization project!
Follow for more home organization tips"
```

**Idea Pin Performance Tips**:
- Idea Pins get distribution in the "Watch" feed (different algorithm)
- Pinterest favors them (keeps users on platform)
- Great for building followers (no external link needed)
- Lower website traffic but higher engagement
- Consider repurposing blog content INTO Idea Pins for reach

## 3. Video Pin

**What it is**: Single video (not carousel), can link out unlike Idea Pins.

### Technical Specifications

**Video Requirements**:
- **File Type**: .mp4, .mov, or .m4v
- **File Size**: Max 2GB
- **Duration**: 4 seconds to 15 minutes (30 seconds - 2 minutes optimal)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square) or 2:3 or 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution**: Minimum 720p (1080p recommended)
- **Encoding**: H.264 or H.265

**Text Limits** (same as Standard Pin):
- **Title**: 100 characters
- **Description**: 500 characters

**Key Feature**: CAN link out (unlike Idea Pins)

### When to Use Video Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Recipe videos (sped-up cooking process)
- Before/after transformations
- Product demonstrations
- Workout routines
- DIY projects with motion
- Quick tips (15-60 seconds)
- Driving traffic with video content

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Long tutorials (use Idea Pin with chapters)
- Content that needs multiple steps shown separately
- Static content (use Standard Pin)

### Optimization Best Practices

**Video Design**:
- First 3 seconds must hook (users scroll fast)
- Add captions (80% watch without sound)
- Show finished result in first frame (thumbnail)
- Keep branding subtle (logo watermark)
- Vertical or square format (no horizontal)

**Video Pacing**:
- Fast cuts (attention span is short)
- Speed up process footage (2x-4x)
- Show key moments, skip boring parts
- End with finished result + CTA

**Thumbnail Strategy**:
- Pinterest auto-generates thumbnail (first frame)
- First frame should be eye-catching and clear
- Include text overlay on first frame
- Show finished result, not mid-process

**Title & Description**:
- Same strategy as Standard Pins
- Keyword-rich, specific, front-loaded
- Mention "video" or "tutorial" if relevant
- Include what's shown in the video

**Example Video Pin**:
```
VIDEO: 45-second sped-up clip of making chocolate chip cookies, from mixing to fresh from oven

THUMBNAIL (First Frame): Finished cookies on cooling rack, text overlay "Easy Chocolate Chip Cookies"

TITLE: "Easy Chocolate Chip Cookies Recipe - No Chilling Required"

DESCRIPTION: "Quick chocolate chip cookies ready in 25 minutes. Soft, chewy centers with crispy edges. Brown butter for extra flavor. Full recipe with ingredient list and baking tips on blog."
```

## 4. Product Pin (Rich Pin)

**What it is**: Special pin format that pulls product data from your website (price, availability, description).

### Technical Specifications

**Requirements**:
- Must have product metadata on your website
- Uses Schema.org markup or Pinterest Product feed
- Verified website/domain
- Active product URL (not sold out)

**Rich Pin Data Includes**:
- Product title
- Current price (auto-updates!)
- Availability (in stock / out of stock)
- Product description
- "Shop" button

**Image Requirements** (same as Standard Pin):
- 1000 x 1500px recommended
- Product clearly visible
- Lifestyle photo OR product-only shot

### When to Use Product Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- E-commerce products
- Physical goods for sale
- Showcasing pricing
- Items that change price/availability
- Shopping-intent content

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Services (use Article Pin)
- Digital products without physical form
- Affiliate products (not your website)
- Out-of-stock items

### Optimization Best Practices

**Image Strategy**:
- Show product in use (lifestyle shot) OR clean product shot
- Include scale reference (person, room, other objects)
- Multiple angles if product is complex
- Show color/finish accurately
- Include packaging if relevant to purchase

**Product Data Optimization**:
- Keep product title clear and keyword-rich
- Include brand, product type, and key feature
- Price should be competitive (users compare)
- Keep in-stock items prioritized

**Rich Pin Setup**:
1. Add Schema.org Product markup to your site
2. Validate with Pinterest Rich Pin Validator
3. Apply for Rich Pins through Pinterest
4. Auto-updates after approval

**Example Product Pin**:
```
IMAGE: Scandinavian-style accent chair in a living room setting

RICH PIN DATA (Auto-Pulled):
- Title: "Mid-Century Modern Accent Chair - Linen Upholstery"
- Price: $289.99
- Availability: In Stock

DESCRIPTION: "Comfortable accent chair for small living rooms. 28-inch width fits tight spaces. Tapered wood legs, neutral beige linen. Assembly required. Perfect for apartments, reading nooks, or bedroom seating."
```

## 5. Article Pin (Rich Pin)

**What it is**: Rich Pin format for blog posts, articles, and editorial content.

### Technical Specifications

**Requirements**:
- Blog post or article URL
- Uses Open Graph or Schema.org Article markup
- Verified website
- Includes headline, author, and description

**Rich Pin Data Includes**:
- Article headline
- Author name
- Story description
- Publication/website name

**Image Requirements**:
- Featured image from the article
- 1000 x 1500px recommended
- Should represent article content

### When to Use Article Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Blog post promotion
- Tutorials with detailed instructions
- Listicles
- How-to guides
- Editorial content
- Authority-building content

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Products for sale (use Product Pin)
- Simple inspiration content
- Non-article destinations

### Optimization Best Practices

**Image Selection**:
- Use article featured image
- Text overlay with headline or key benefit
- Show finished result or key visual
- Make it "save-worthy" (users bookmark for later)

**Headline Strategy**:
- Rich Pin pulls your article H1 tag
- Make sure H1 is keyword-optimized
- Include numbers, benefits, specifics
- Front-load primary keyword

**Description**:
- Write Pinterest-specific description (don't rely on auto-pull)
- Expand on headline with keywords
- Mention what's in the article
- Include why someone should click

**Example Article Pin**:
```
IMAGE: Organized pantry with labeled containers, text overlay "12 Pantry Organization Ideas"

RICH PIN DATA (Auto-Pulled):
- Headline: "12 Pantry Organization Ideas That Maximize Storage Space"
- Author: Jane Smith
- Site: HomeOrganizingPro.com

DESCRIPTION: "Transform a messy pantry into an organized system. Includes clear container recommendations, labeling tips, and shelf organization strategies. Perfect for small pantries and large families. Free printable labels included."
```

## 6. Collection Pin

**What it is**: Pin that showcases multiple products in a single image with tagged product details.

### Technical Specifications

**Requirements**:
- Catalog upload to Pinterest
- Verified merchant account
- Tagged products in the image

**Features**:
- Users tap on products to see details
- Direct link to purchase
- Multiple products per pin

**Image Requirements**:
- Products clearly visible
- Multiple items shown (2-6 products ideal)
- Clean, organized layout

### When to Use Collection Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Product bundles
- "Get the look" fashion/decor
- Multiple coordinating products
- Room designs with shoppable items
- Outfit flatlays

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Single products (use Product Pin)
- Services
- Non-shoppable content

**Example Collection Pin**:
```
IMAGE: Living room with sofa, coffee table, rug, and pillows

TAGGED PRODUCTS:
- Mid-Century Sofa ($899)
- Round Coffee Table ($249)
- Jute Area Rug ($179)
- Linen Throw Pillows ($39 each)

DESCRIPTION: "Complete Scandinavian living room under $1,500. Shop this look with tagged products. Neutral color palette, minimalist style, perfect for small apartments."
```

## 7. Carousel Pin (Standard)

**What it is**: Multiple images in a single pin, users swipe through (2-5 images).

### Technical Specifications

**Requirements**:
- 2-5 images per pin
- All images same size (1000 x 1500px)
- Single title and description (not per-image)

**Use Cases**:
- Multiple product views (front/back/detail)
- Before/after comparisons
- Color variations
- Step-by-step with images
- Multiple outfit ideas with same pieces

### When to Use Carousel Pins

✅ **Best for**:
- Showing product details
- Before/after/during
- Multiple angles
- Variations (colors, styles)
- Related ideas

❌ **Not ideal for**:
- Tutorials (use Idea Pin for better format)
- Unrelated images
- Content better as separate pins

**Example Carousel Pin**:
```
IMAGE 1: Front view of a dress
IMAGE 2: Back view showing detail
IMAGE 3: Dress styled with accessories
IMAGE 4: Dress in different color
IMAGE 5: Size chart

TITLE: "Midi Wrap Dress - Summer Workwear for Women"

DESCRIPTION: "Versatile wrap dress in 4 colors. Swipe for details, styling ideas, and size chart. Perfect for office, interviews, or business casual. Wrinkle-resistant fabric, machine washable. Sizes XS-3X."
```

## Pinterest SEO: Cross-Format Strategy

### Keyword Placement Hierarchy

**Most Important** (weighted heavily by algorithm):
1. **Pin Title** (first 50 characters)
2. **Image Text Overlay** (if readable)
3. **Pin Description** (first 100 characters)
4. **Board Title**
5. **Alt Text**

**Less Important** (still matters):
6. **Hashtags** (2-4 relevant)
7. **Board Description**
8. **Account Name/Bio**

### Hashtag Strategy

**How Pinterest hashtags work** (different from Instagram):
- Used for search, not discovery feed
- 2-4 hashtags ideal (not 20-30 like Instagram)
- Should be searchable terms, not brand names
- Combine broad + specific

**Example**:
```
For a small bathroom organization pin:
✅ #SmallBathroom #BathroomOrganization
❌ #Bathroom #BathroomIdeas #BathroomDecor #BathroomDesign #SmallBathroom
#TinyBathroom #BathroomStorage #BathroomHacks #OrganizingIdeas
```

**Best practices**:
- Place hashtags at end of description
- Use same hashtags as category keywords
- Don't repeat words already in title/description
- 1 broad + 1-2 specific works well

## Save vs. Click Optimization

Different content goals require different strategies.

### Optimize for SAVES (Maximize Reach)

**Goal**: Get pinned to many boards, spread widely, build awareness

**Best for**:
- Inspiration content (home decor, fashion, travel)
- Aspirational pins
- Board-building content
- Brand awareness

**Strategy**:
- Image is the hero (text overlay minimal)
- Description is aspirational + keyword-rich
- Multiple use cases mentioned
- Board-worthy aesthetic
- Evergreen timing

**Example**:
```
IMAGE: Stunning Scandinavian living room, minimal text

TITLE: "Scandinavian Living Room Ideas - Minimalist Decor"

DESCRIPTION: "Modern Scandinavian living room with neutral colors, natural textures, and minimalist furniture. Perfect for apartments, small spaces, or anyone who loves clean, cozy design. IKEA and budget-friendly options included."

(Goal: User saves to "Living Room Ideas," "Dream Home," "Scandinavian Style" boards for future reference)
```

### Optimize for CLICKS (Drive Traffic)

**Goal**: Get clicks to your website/blog/product page

**Best for**:
- Tutorial content
- Recipes
- Blog posts
- Product pages
- How-to guides

**Strategy**:
- Text overlay promises specific value
- Description teases what's on the blog
- Clear CTA ("full tutorial")
- Curiosity gap (show result, need to click for steps)
- Benefit-focused

**Example**:
```
IMAGE: Before/after closet organization, text "How to Organize a Small Closet in 3 Hours"

TITLE: "Small Closet Organization in 3 Hours - Step-by-Step Tutorial"

DESCRIPTION: "Complete closet organization guide under $50. Dollar Tree products, no drilling required. Step-by-step photos, shopping list with prices, and measurements included. Perfect for bedroom closets 4-6 feet wide."

(Goal: User clicks for the full tutorial, supplies list, and photos)
```

### Optimize for BOTH (Balanced)

**Strategy**:
- Saveable aesthetic
- Clear value proposition
- Keyword-rich for search
- Gentle CTA for clicks
- Works as reference AND resource

**Example**:
```
IMAGE: Beautiful organized pantry with labeled containers

TITLE: "Pantry Organization Ideas with Free Printable Labels"

DESCRIPTION: "Transform pantry chaos into an organized system. Includes container recommendations, labeling strategy, and shelf arrangement tips. Free printable labels in 3 styles. Perfect for small pantries and busy families. Full guide on blog."

(Goal: User saves for "Pantry Ideas" board AND clicks for free labels)
```

## Performance Metrics by Format

### What Pinterest Measures

- **Impressions**: How many times pin appears
- **Saves**: How many times pinned to boards
- **Clicks**: Outbound clicks to destination
- **Closeups**: Pin opened for detail view
- **Comments**: Engagement (rare on Pinterest)

### Benchmarks by Format

**Standard Pins**:
- Save rate: 1-5% (higher for inspiration content)
- Click rate: 0.5-2% (higher for tutorials)
- Lifespan: 3-6 months active distribution, years of passive

**Idea Pins**:
- Higher engagement (native format)
- Lower clicks (no outbound link)
- Better for follower growth
- Shorter active lifespan (1-3 months)

**Video Pins**:
- Higher impressions (algorithm favors)
- Lower saves (harder to reference)
- Good click rates if CTA is clear
- Medium lifespan (2-4 months)

**Product/Rich Pins**:
- Higher closeup rate (price checking)
- Better click-through (shopping intent)
- Longer lifespan (evergreen products)

## The Bottom Line: Format Selection Matrix

**Choose your format based on**:

| Content Type | Best Format | Why |
|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Blog post promotion | Article Pin | Rich metadata, credibility |
| Product sale | Product Pin | Price, availability, shop button |
| Step-by-step tutorial | Idea Pin | Multi-page, keeps on platform, engages |
| Recipe | Video Pin or Standard | Video shows process, Standard for save |
| Inspiration | Standard Pin | Clean aesthetic, board-worthy |
| Before/after | Carousel or Video | Shows transformation |
| Multiple products | Collection Pin | Shoppable, coordinated |
| Quick tip | Video Pin (under 30s) | Engaging, shareable |
| Long-form guide | Article Pin | Drives blog traffic |

**General rule**: If you want traffic to your site, use Standard, Video, or Article Pins. If you want Pinterest-native engagement, use Idea Pins.

All formats benefit from: keyword optimization, vertical images, text overlays, specific value props, and authentic, non-promotional language.
